THEC1LAB privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026. This policy covers THEC1LAB (trading as C1 Lab), an independent UK art print studio run by a sole trader, the THEC1LAB Etsy shop, our social accounts (@thec1lab on Instagram and TikTok, and our Pinterest), and the small tools we run for them. Those tools only ever connect to our own accounts. They are not offered to anyone else.

Etsy orders and certificates

  • When you buy from our Etsy shop, Etsy shares your name, email address and order details with us so we can deliver your order. We use them only to fulfil the order, email your numbered certificate of authenticity (and, for limited editions, your collector copy), and answer your messages.
  • Certificates are sent by email only, as a PDF. No names, email addresses or order numbers are published anywhere.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Etsy. We never see your card details.
  • We keep order and certificate records only as long as we need them for fulfilment, certificate checks and UK tax records.

Instagram (@thec1lab)

  • Our Instagram tool uses Instagram's official API, signed in through Instagram's own login, to publish our posts, read the insights for our own posts, and read and reply to comments and direct messages sent to @thec1lab.
  • When you comment on one of our posts, we read your username and comment so we can reply, for example to a puzzle answer or a "where can I buy this?". The comment text is checked by an automated classifier (see "Services we use") to decide whether it needs a person. Anything that isn't a simple compliment, shop question or puzzle answer is passed to us to answer by hand.
  • Direct messages are never answered automatically. They are forwarded to our own private workspace so we can reply ourselves.
  • We don't collect data about your followers, your other posts or your profile, and we don't build profiles of people.

C1 Lab Pin Scheduler (Pinterest)

  • The app is used only by C1 Lab, to publish Pins of our own artwork to our own Pinterest boards and to read the performance of those Pins. It is not offered to other Pinterest users.
  • It reads only our boards, our Pins and their analytics. It does not collect, store or process data about other Pinterest users, their followers or their content.
  • To disconnect the app, we revoke its access in Pinterest's settings and delete the stored token.

TikTok and other platforms

  • We post our own artwork to our own TikTok account, and read the public view numbers for our own videos. We do not collect data about other users.
  • Where a platform's official sign-in is used (OAuth), we never ask for or store a password. Access tokens are stored encrypted in our private secrets manager on our own server, never published or put in source code, and deleted when we disconnect.

Services we use

  • Etsy: shop, payments and order data.
  • Resend: delivers the certificate emails.
  • OpenRouter: sends the text of a public comment on our posts, with no username, to a classification model that labels it (for example "compliment" or "question"). It returns a label, not text, and the comment isn't used for advertising.
  • Discord: our private workspace, where comments and messages that need a person are sent to us.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

To have any data we hold about you deleted, including Instagram comments or messages we've stored, send us an Etsy message (etsy.com/shop/THEC1LAB) or a direct message to @thec1lab on Instagram, saying what you'd like removed. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm when it's done. Records we must keep for UK tax law are kept only for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, using the contacts above. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
